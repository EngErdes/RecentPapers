## 📝 要約

Many studies have reported that incorporating these signals in remote communication had positive effects in various situations, such as collaborative tasks [4], [5], [6], [7], online learning [8], [9], collaborative gameplay [10], live performances [11], and evaluations of social connectedness [12], [13].

Among the signals examined in these previous studies, heart rate and facial expression are the most practical forms of data to send.

This has led to the incorporation of heart rate data in the operation of consumer devices, including an air
conditioner that measures heart rate in a non-contact manner and uses the data to set the room temperature and airflow (Kirigamine Z-series, 2023, Mitsubishi Electric Corporation)
